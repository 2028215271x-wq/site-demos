Roofing Questions

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Signs include missing shingles, water stains on ceilings, granules in gutters, or a roof over 20 years old. We'll inspect it free and tell you straight — no upselling.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every estimate is free, written, and good for 30 days. Ken personally walks the roof and photos are included.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most Tulsa homes are completed in 1-2 days, weather permitting. We clean up completely — magnet sweep included.

Are you licensed and insured?

Fully licensed and insured in Oklahoma. Certificate available on request — just ask.
